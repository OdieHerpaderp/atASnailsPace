meshes/shell.glb

One shell, and it is the reference model's own geometry rather than a coil wound here: references/snailRef.glb is a scanned or sculpted snail shell, and a swept logarithmic spiral cut from three numbers in this folder was never going to be that. This page takes its first mesh and makes it a piece of the county, which is four things and none of them optional:

- **a color attribute**, because a glb without one draws pure white - three substitutes white for an absent vertex colour - and race.js multiplies the racer's own shell colour into it. The ramp is near white on purpose: the hue is the tint's and never the model's.
- **no embedded images.** The reference carries two, on a pbrSpecularGlossiness extension, and a texture baked into a glb is one the game cannot swap or leave out. The maps come out of meshes/tex/ by name instead, through maps.js.
- **a uv on the county's tile**, so shell-n's growth lines land at a size rather than at whatever the reference's wrap happened to be, and
- **the middle of its own bounding box at the origin**, so shellG's position is the middle of the shell and the same number means the same thing on any machine.

And it is not double-sided. The reference's material says doubleSided and its signed volume is positive, so the geometry faces out and the flag was covering for something else. The county is single-sided, because a back face is a hole at every angle the field is seen from.

snail.glb carries a shell of its own, cut from this one, and the game swaps it in the moment a racer is built.
